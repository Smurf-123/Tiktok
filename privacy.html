Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, non-commercial automation tool used solely to publish video content to the TikTok account of its owner/operator. It is not a public-facing service and does not process data on behalf of third-party users.

What data is accessed

This application accesses only the TikTok account information necessary to publish video content via the TikTok Content Posting API (e.g. basic profile information and video upload permissions), authorized directly by the account owner through TikTok's OAuth login flow.

How data is used

Access tokens are used exclusively to upload and publish video content to the authorized TikTok account. No data collected through this process is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Data storage

Access and refresh tokens are stored securely as encrypted secrets within the application's private infrastructure and are not accessible to any third party.

Data deletion

The account owner may revoke this application's access at any time via TikTok's account settings, which immediately invalidates all associated tokens.
